The ATG pillars.

01

Invest

Real-World Application

At ATG, education translates into action. Members actively manage the club’s $25,000 portfolio using a variety of long-only strategies.

02

Develop

Professional Development

Breaking into high finance roles requires more than a solid technical understanding. ATG provides structured support for our members to navigate the recruiting timelines and land top offers.

03

Belong

Community

ATG cultivates a strong community of people with an interest in Finance, spanning various hobbies, backgrounds, and involvement at Michigan.
